## Entries written after the pan-cancer results were viewed (2026-11-25T01:39:58Z)

2. **Reporting of non-eligible types and completion of S5 (reporting/implementation, after results).** No primary or secondary analysis was changed. (a) `P02_pancan_analysis.py` also writes per-type statistics for the 12 non-eligible types. 3. **Post hoc exploratory analyses (2026-11-26T04:02:30Z; after the primary results were viewed, before these analyses were run).** Added in response to an internal referee-style review; none was in the plan and all are reported as exploratory: (a) gene selection repeated inside each jackknife replicate; (b) strata by number of HM450 probes per promoter (2–3, 4–7, ≥ 8); (c) genes with methylation-only CV-R² > 0.3 and their recurrence across types; (d) a two-state methylation model (Otsu threshold per gene, both groups ≥ 3 tumors); (e) ATAC-seq quality (mean TSS enrichment) against D by type. Code: `P05_posthoc_robustness.py`. The Basal-versus-luminal analysis of the breast paper is moved to the supplement as exploratory; the paper's framing changes to lead with what was pre-specified and held in every type (methylation adds nothing given accessibility; methylation alone does not predict expression), with the failed R2 criterion reported in the abstract.

4. **Post hoc exploratory analysis (2026-11-26T04:10:29Z; after the results of entry 3 were viewed, before this analysis was run).** (f) Single-predictor mismatched-promoter null: the methylation-only and accessibility-only models refitted with each gene given the methylation or accessibility of a random gene from the same expression-mean decile (100 draws), to show what CV-R² an unrelated predictor gives at each type's sample size. Code: `P07_single_predictor_null.py`. Exploratory.

5. **Corrections and post hoc analyses after an internal pre-submission review (2026-11-26T04:49:29Z; after all earlier results were viewed, before these analyses were run).** (g) CV-R² as defined in the plan (1 − PRESS/SST, SST about the mean of all tumors) is not zero for a model that predicts each held-out tumor by the mean of the others; that model scores 1 − (n/(n − 1))². The primary criteria R1–R3 are kept as pre-specified, but statements about whether a model predicts better than the cancer-type mean are re-based: CV-R² is also reported relative to the leave-one-out covariate-only model (intercept only for non-breast types; intercept and array platform for breast), i.e. 1 − PRESS/PRESS₀, which is the scale already used by the pooled within-type analysis (S2). (h) The plan's S1 asked for a random-effects pooled median ρ; the code computed a tumor-weighted mean instead. The pooled value is now computed by DerSimonian–Laird with delete-one-tumor jackknife standard errors. (i) Hartung–Knapp–Sidik–Jonkman intervals and 95% prediction intervals for pooled D and G, as a sensitivity analysis for k = 9. (j) A comparator for the recurrence analysis: the same random-gene null applied to accessibility-informative genes (CV-R² of accessibility alone > 0.3). (k) The fraction of genes for which methylation outperforms accessibility by more than 0.1 (already reported in the text) is labelled exploratory. The paper's framing, led by the co-primary criterion R3 and by the pooled within-type analysis rather than by R1 as in the plan's working title, was chosen after the results were seen (entry 3) and is stated as such in the manuscript. Code: `P08_review_corrections.py`.
